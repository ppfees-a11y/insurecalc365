About InsureCalc365

Independent free insurance math tools and transparent educational explanations.

Our purpose

InsureCalc365 helps readers make sense of insurance arithmetic: premiums, deductibles, cost sharing, coverage gaps, and household protection needs. The tools are free and do not require registration.

The site focuses on understanding figures you already have, not selling insurance or promising a quote.

Editorial approach

We use clear formulas, labeled assumptions, and worked examples rather than pretending that hypothetical figures are current insurance prices. Every calculation has limitations, explained alongside the tool and in our methodology.

Insurance laws, products, and contract terms vary. For binding answers about your own policy, consult the policy and your insurer or licensed advisor.
