Insurance terms explained

Insurance terms can look similar while affecting your costs in different ways. These definitions describe common U.S. usage; your policy documents control the actual terms.

Premium

The amount paid to keep a policy in force, often monthly, semiannually, or annually. Paying premiums does not ordinarily satisfy your deductible. Some insurers charge installment fees.

Deductible

The amount you must bear before a policy pays certain covered costs. Some deductibles apply per claim; others apply during a policy year. Percentage deductibles may be based on an insured value rather than the amount of a claim.

Copayment (copay)

A fixed amount for a covered service under specified health-plan rules, such as $30 for an office visit. A copay is not necessarily subject to the same deductible or coinsurance treatment as other services.

Coinsurance

A percentage of allowed covered charges paid by the member after applicable deductible rules. At 20% coinsurance, a $1,000 eligible post-deductible charge would produce $200 of patient coinsurance before any applicable out-of-pocket cap.

Allowed amount

The amount a health plan recognizes for a covered service, which can differ from a provider's billed charge. Network status and plan rules matter.

Out-of-pocket maximum

The maximum qualifying cost sharing a covered member pays during a specified benefit period under a health plan. Premiums and many noncovered or out-of-network costs generally do not count. Check whether individual and family maximums differ.

Coverage limit

A ceiling on what a policy may pay for a specified coverage, subject to its terms. A sublimit can cap a particular type of loss below the main policy limit.

Exclusion

A loss, event, service, or circumstance the policy does not cover. A calculator cannot make an excluded claim payable.

Replacement cost versus actual cash value

Replacement-cost coverage generally considers the cost to replace damaged property with comparable property, subject to policy conditions. Actual cash value commonly accounts for depreciation; exact definitions vary.

Endorsement

An amendment that adds, removes, or changes policy coverage or conditions. Riders are similar additions often used with life insurance.

In-network versus out-of-network

Health-plan providers can have different negotiated rates and cost-sharing rules depending on network status. Some plans have limited or no nonemergency out-of-network coverage.

Where to confirm definitions

Use your insurer's policy and benefits documents first. General consumer resources include the National Association of Insurance Commissioners and HealthCare.gov glossary. These outside resources do not endorse InsureCalc365.
